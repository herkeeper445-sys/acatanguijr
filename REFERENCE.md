# Portfolio Design Reference

Primary visual reference:

https://sample-nextjs-portfolio.vercel.app/?utm_source=chatgpt.com

Public repository used only to understand page structure and interaction ideas:

https://github.com/iammiracle01/sample-next-js-portfolio

## Visual ideas preserved in Ananias' version

- Dark navy / black futuristic background
- White + purple + cyan highlight palette
- Floating navigation
- Animated spotlights in the hero
- Animated text entrance
- Large headline and CTA
- Bento-style information grid
- Strong project/sample cards with interactive previews
- Experience section
- Step-by-step work approach section
- Split contact section with details on the left and a form on the right
- Smooth scroll/reveal animation
- Responsive mobile layout

## Customizations for Ananias

- General Administrative Virtual Assistant positioning
- Profile photo with orbiting/moving particle dots behind the portrait
- Gmail, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, phone, and Iriga City location
- Practice samples clearly labeled as self-created / portfolio practice
- Spreadsheet, calendar, inbox, meeting minutes, research, itinerary, Drive organization, customer support, SOP, task board, Canva, APEX, and n8n learning-project samples
- Printable resume page

Do not copy the sample owner's identity, wording, project claims, or personal information.
